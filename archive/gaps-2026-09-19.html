This cycle's coverage gap report

An honest account of where the index is thin, so nobody mistakes an empty search result for "this doesn't exist." Updated 2026-09-19.

Known gaps as of this cycle

  • Grade skew. Coverage is still weighted toward Grade 12 / FET past papers. This cycle added all eleven official Home Language Paper 2 and Paper 3 papers (with memos) for Grade 12, which deepens language coverage but not the lower grades.
  • Graded readers are on hold. Grade 1 readers for all eleven languages are not listed this cycle: our link checks on DBE reader addresses did not return the expected file, so we have left them out until they can be confirmed. Grade 3 readers are not yet indexed in any language.
  • One Big Book listing removed pending review. The isiNdebele Big Book 1 entry shares a download address with a Grade 1 reader on the DBE's own index page, so we can't tell which title it really is.
  • Official Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy and Physical Sciences papers. The DBE's 2025 November page lists these, but we link to provincial or SACAI copies for some of them. The DBE originals are a priority for next cycle.
  • IEB coverage is weak. The IEB does not publish past papers openly, so our only IEB entries come via a third-party rehost (trust level C). We have not found a trust-A source.
  • Cambridge IGCSE is index-level, not paper-level, and sourced from a third-party rehost (trust level C).
If you know a trust-A source we're missing — especially for IEB or Cambridge — tell us.
