## Linked third-party resources: not ours to license

Every textbook, past paper, worksheet, video, or other resource this site
links to remains the property of its original publisher — the Department
of Basic Education, a provincial education department, a university, a
commercial publisher, an OER project, or an individual educator — under
whatever terms that publisher has set.

- We do not claim ownership of any linked resource.
- We do not grant you any rights to a linked resource beyond what its
  original publisher grants.
- Where a linked resource carries its own open licence (e.g. Siyavula's
  CC BY textbooks), that resource's own licence governs your use of it —
  check the `licence` field we've recorded, then confirm on the source
  itself before relying on it.
- Where a linked resource's licence is `unknown`, treat it as fully
  copyrighted and do not redistribute it — our labelling defaults to
  closed, not open, whenever we haven't found an explicit licence
  statement.

## If you believe something is misclassified or shouldn't be indexed

If a resource is indexed here that shouldn't be (incorrect licence label,
copyright concern, or content that turns out to be an unauthorised
mirror), open an issue or contact the maintainer. We prioritise removing
or correcting these over any other maintenance task.
